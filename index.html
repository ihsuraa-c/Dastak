How it works

Four simple steps, from your phone to your doorstep.

  1. Tell us what's wrong

    Describe your symptoms in the app. A doctor reviews the summary before we dispatch.

  2. We check for red flags

    If it looks serious, we connect you to 108/112. If not, we send the unit with the right equipment.

  3. Examined at your door

    A Mobile Clinical Associate checks your vitals while a registered doctor joins by video.

  4. Plan and follow-up

    Your prescription and follow-up plan arrive in the app, and we check in afterwards.

What we treat, and what we don't

Being clear about this is part of keeping you safe.

We can help with

  • Fever and common infections
  • Dehydration
  • Minor injuries and wound care
  • Basic breathing complaints, like cough and mild wheeze
  • Blood sugar and blood pressure concerns
  • Maternal and newborn check-ups
  • Check-ups after hospital discharge

Call 108 or 112 instead

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Signs of stroke, like a drooping face or slurred speech
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Heavy bleeding that won't stop
  • Severe injury
  • Pregnancy emergencies
  • Unconsciousness

If you see any of these, don't wait. Call 108 or 112 now.
